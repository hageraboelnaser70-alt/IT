Help Center

Find answers to common questions about our bookstore.

1. How can I browse books?

Visit the Home Page to explore the available books.

2. How can I see book details?

Click the Details button under any book to see its information.

3. How can I buy a book?

Open the book details, select the quantity, and click Buy Now.

4. Do I need to create an account?

Yes. Follow the Create Account page before continuing to Login.

5. How can I create an account?

Enter your name, email, password, and confirm your password in the registration form.

6. What should I do if my passwords do not match?

Return to the Create Account form and enter the same password in both fields.

7. How can I review my order?

After logging in successfully, you will be directed to the Purchase Review page.

8. How is the total price calculated?

The total price is calculated by multiplying the price of one book by the selected quantity.

9. How can I confirm my purchase?

Check the order details and click the green Confirm Purchase button.

10. How can I return to the Home Page?

Click Home in the navigation bar or Home Page on the Thank You page.

11. How can I contact the bookstore?

Open the Contact page and complete the contact form.

12. Is this a real online payment system?

No. This is a frontend educational project and does not process real payments.
